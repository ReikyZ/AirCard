Privacy Policy for AirCard

Developer: Rui Zhang (ReikyZ)
Bundle ID: com.reikyz.aircard
Effective Date: September 24, 2026

Overview

AirCard is a Mac utility that allows you to customize the appearance of your Apple Wallet and transit card skins. This privacy policy explains how AirCard handles your data and protects your privacy.

Data Collection

AirCard does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. All customization and processing occurs entirely on your device.

No Account Required

The App Store version of AirCard does not require you to create an account or log in. You can use all features without providing any personal information.

Local File Access

When you select images to customize your wallet cards, you may grant AirCard permission to access those specific files on your device. These files remain on your device and are not transmitted to any server or third party. AirCard only accesses files you explicitly choose.

On-Device Processing

All image processing and card customization happens locally on your Mac. The App Store version of AirCard does not communicate with external servers or upload your data to the cloud.

No Backend Service

The developer does not operate a backend service that collects personal information from the App Store version of AirCard. There is no remote server receiving data from your use of the app.

No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising

AirCard does not include third-party analytics SDKs or advertising frameworks in the App Store build. Your usage patterns and behavior are not tracked or shared with any third parties.

Data Sales

AirCard does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone. Since no personal data is collected, there is nothing to sell or share.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
